# Behind Closed Doors, Beyond Numbers

**PLS 419 final project | Kazakhstan | Qorgau AI (concept prototype)**

This GitHub Pages website accompanies the research paper **“Behind Closed Doors, Beyond Numbers.”** It examines the causes and persistence of domestic violence against women in Kazakhstan, the barriers to seeking help, and a proposed AI-assisted information service called **Qorgau AI**.

## Research question

> What causes and sustains domestic violence against women in Kazakhstan, and how could an AI-assisted information service reduce the barriers to help that these causes create?

## Structure

1. **The problem:** social stigma, underreporting, the changing legal context, and the limits of headline figures.
2. **Background and data:** interactive regional charts of recorded cases in 2017 and 2025, disaggregated by age; discussion of differences in reporting, geography and data comparability.
3. **Current research:** social norms and shame, economic dependence, intergenerational transmission, legal deterrence and implementation, unequal geography, and barriers to disclosure.
4. **Qorgau AI and its costs:** an illustrative multilingual information navigator, the proposed functions and safety boundaries, an AinoAid comparison, an adjustable pilot budget, ethical risks and a conditional recommendation.

The site reflects the accompanying Word paper, which remains the authoritative written version. Regional totals are **recorded cases, not prevalence estimates**. The 2017 and 2025 tables use different regional boundaries and should not be interpreted as a controlled before-and-after comparison.

## Interactive features

- Switch between the 2017 and 2025 regional recorded-case charts.
- Select a region, language and information pathway in the **illustrative** Qorgau AI interface.
- Explore a hypothetical first-year pilot budget and adjust region coverage, monthly users and human-review staffing.

**Important:** Qorgau AI is not a live domestic violence support service. The prototype does not connect to organizations, provide crisis intervention, accept reports or offer personalized legal advice. Its directory, translations and legal information would require independent verification, professional review and privacy testing before deployment. The budget is a planning illustration, **not a vendor quotation**.

## Run locally

Open `index.html` in a browser. No framework, package manager or build step is required. Keep `assets/wounding-numbers.jpg` alongside the site if you want the research illustration to display.

## Publish with GitHub Pages

1. Upload `index.html`, `README.md` and the `assets/` folder to a GitHub repository.
2. Open **Settings → Pages**.
3. Under **Build and deployment**, select **Deploy from a branch**.
4. Select branch `main` and folder `/ (root)`, then save.
5. Open the Pages URL shown by GitHub once deployment finishes.

## Sources and research integrity

The accompanying Word paper contains the research discussion and references, including Bureau of National Statistics / General Prosecutor's Office regional statistics, UN Women, Schitaetsya.kz, and studies by Karabay et al., Snajdr, Mussabekova et al., Rani and Bonu, Zhubandykova et al., Sarykulov et al., and Kainazarova et al. (Check the paper's bibliography for complete citation details.)

Figures should not be read as proof of causation or as estimates of unreported violence. The project was developed with AI assistance, with the student responsible for research choices, verification and final interpretation.

## Safety

Do not enter identifying or sensitive personal information into this demo. The site is an academic illustration, not an emergency resource. A quick-exit button cannot erase browsing history or protect a device monitored by an abuser.
